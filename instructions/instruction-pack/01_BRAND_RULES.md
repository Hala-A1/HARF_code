# Brand Style — Naming, Grammar and Formatting

> **Active scope:** Standard brand and writing mechanics for every Copywriter request.
> **Source:** `e_CX Style Guide.pdf` (10 pages, 30 September 2026 upload).
> **Precedence:** These rules apply globally. More specific legal, channel, component, or supplied campaign requirements take precedence when they explicitly differ.

## Brand name

- Use etisalat or etisalat UAE as the approved current English company names
- Use an ampersand only when it is part of an approved product or entity name; the company names etisalat and etisalat UAE contain no ampersand. Write `and` elsewhere

## Abbreviations and acronyms

- Explain an uncommon abbreviation or acronym on first use when space allows: `B1G1 (Buy-1-get-1)`
- Do not use periods in acronyms: `UAE`, not `U.A.E.`
- Prefer familiar abbreviations and acronyms

## Bullet points

- Capitalise the first word after each bullet
- Do not add a period to a fragment or a single-sentence bullet
- Use periods when a bullet contains two or more sentences

## Calls to action

- Start CTAs with an imperative verb, such as `Discover`, `Explore`, or `Buy`
- Keep the action specific, short, and consistent with the customer's next step

## Capitalisation

- Capitalise proper nouns, including names, organisations, plans, places, and business units
- Capitalise months and days of the week, but not seasons
- Do not use capital letters for emphasis: `Free`, not `FREE`

## Conjunctions and contractions

- Starting a sentence with `And` or `But` is acceptable when used sparingly
- Use natural contractions such as `don't` and `we're`

## Currency

- Use `D` as the source guide's default currency symbol and place it immediately before the number: `D250`
- Use standard abbreviations for other currencies
- State whether a price includes VAT when space allows and the fact is known
- When supplied copy or a current approved terminology source requires `AED`, preserve the supplied approved form

## Dates and times

- Use day-month-year order: `31 July 2025`
- Avoid slash-separated dates unless space is tight
- Use the 12-hour clock with `am` or `pm`: `9am`, `9:00am`
- In push notifications and app interfaces, abbreviate dates when needed: `Offer ends 31 Aug`
- Include a time zone only when relevant

## Emirati culture

- Avoid slang, idioms, and humour that may not translate well
- Respect cultural norms and values
- Avoid references that assume a Western point of view

## Greetings and personalisation

- When a name is unavailable, use `Hello there,`
- When a name is available, use `Hello $FIRST_NAME$` or `$FIRST_NAME$`
- For Emirati and Business audiences, use `Dear $FIRST_NAME$`
- Use the customer's name when available, but no more than once per message
- Address the reader directly

## Hyphens and dashes

- Use a hyphen for compound words: `hassle-free`, `in-app support`
- Use an en dash for ranges: `10am–2pm`
- An en dash with a space on each side may mark emphasis or a longer pause: `Register – we'll do the rest.`
- Use dashes sparingly, especially in SMS and other tight spaces

## Measurements

- Use the metric system
- Use numerals for measurements: `5GB`, `100Mbps`
- Use standard abbreviations and no space between the number and unit: `500MB`

## Numbers

- Spell out one to nine and use numerals from 10 onward
- Always use numerals with units or data: `3GB`, `5 minutes`

## Punctuation

- Use familiar punctuation
- Use exclamation marks only in SMS, and sparingly
- Use ellipses (`...`) sparingly
- Use one space after a period
- Use the serial comma when it improves clarity
- Do not end a one-sentence headline with a period
- Use periods when a headline contains two sentences
- Keep headlines to 10 words or fewer

## Phone numbers

- Separate phone-number groups with spaces rather than periods or hyphens: `05 677 11508`

## Spelling

- Use British English spelling: `optimise`, `organise`, `colour`, `centre`

## URLs

- Keep URLs short and relevant
- Use approved shortened links for SMS and push notifications
- Hyperlink only the most relevant one to three words, not a full sentence

## Sentence length

- Keep sentences under 20 words wherever possible

## Final brand check

Confirm brand naming, capitalisation, currency, dates, units, punctuation, spelling, CTA style, headline length, and sentence length before returning copy.
