# B2B Messaging Examples — Before, After and Arabic Copy

> **Active scope:** B2B Copywriter requests only.
> **Source:** `Copy of RW x e_CX Messaging Bank_B2B_12 December 2025.xlsx`.
> **Use:** Treat `After` and Arabic fields as formatting and tone examples. Treat `Before` fields as anti-patterns. Never reuse prices, dates, benefits, URLs, product details, legal wording, or claims unless they are supplied or verified for the current request.

## Patterns to apply

- Use respectful B2B greetings and professional, direct language
- Lead with the outcome or status, then present essential account, order, service, charge, and next-step details
- Group transactional details under clear labels instead of embedding them in dense paragraphs
- Preserve reference numbers, account numbers, placeholders, plan names, dates, prices, links, and conditions exactly
- Focus on trust, reliability, support, long-term value, and the business relationship
- Use a clear CTA or next step when action is required
- Keep English and Arabic versions equivalent in facts, conditions, urgency, and structure
- Return a polished message in the actual channel format, ready to send
