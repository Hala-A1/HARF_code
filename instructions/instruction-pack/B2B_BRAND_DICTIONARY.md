## Purpose and governance note

This document provides a controlled Brand Dictionary and accepted English terminology for a writing AI agent. It consolidates terminology observed across the three specified etisalat websites and separates exact official names from generic terminology that should be normalized for new content.

The websites are not fully consistent. An AI agent should therefore not treat every published variation as equally acceptable. Each entry should be governed using the status labels below, with current approved product and legal sources taking priority when they conflict with older website wording.
